Our story

Most of what goes in the jar came out of our own garden.

Placeholder, two minutes on a voice memo and we'll write this properly

Paula and Crazy John have been putting up salsa, hot sauce, pickles and cowboy candy out of a home kitchen in Elgin, Texas, growing most of the peppers and produce themselves. What started as too much of a garden harvest turned into a booth, and the booth turned into a following.

Everything is made in small batches under the Texas Cottage Food Law, jarred and labeled by hand, and sold face to face at markets across Central Texas.
